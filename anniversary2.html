A Little Part of Our Story 💌

Some memories are too special to simply leave behind, so I wanted to keep these little pieces of our journey here — somewhere they can stay safe and be remembered whenever we come back to this page.

One Year Letter is a little part of the story created by Elsa, while 365 Reasons to Love You is something created by Hamza. They may be just two PDF files, but behind those pages are thoughts, effort, memories, and little moments that became part of our story.

This page isn't only about looking back at what we made. It's about keeping the little things that might otherwise get forgotten with time. Years from now, we can open these again and remember what we were like, what we thought, and all the little things that mattered to us during this chapter.

So these aren't here just as documents. They're here as little time capsules of our memories — two pieces of a journey that deserve to be kept.

Some things are worth saving, simply because they remind us of a time we never want to forget. ❤️
