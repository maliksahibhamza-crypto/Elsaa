Beginning

I was never really interested in online relationships or friendships. At that time, I mostly stayed in a team with my adopted sister, and eventually, even she stopped coming online. So, I decided that maybe it was time to delete the game.

But then, by coincidence, I thought, “Why not visit a few teams before deleting it, just for fun?”

That small decision led me to a new team called Gangster Life. There were only around seven or eight members, and the leader was Abu Bakar. I talked to him for a while, and he seemed like a good person. We became friends, and eventually, he made me one of the team's deputies.

Slowly, the team started growing. I brought a boy into the team, and I even got him promoted to deputy as well. But after some time, for reasons I don't really remember anymore, I started disliking him and his friends. There may have been a specific reason, but honestly, I don't remember what it was.

There was also something he told me personally. He said, “Hamza, I'm engaged,” even though I had proposed to Aiza, who was my friend. I used to bring Aiza—the girl I considered like a sister—into the team with me. At first, none of that was really an issue. But later, that same boy proposed to her.

Eventually, things started falling apart. I had arguments with Aiza, with that boy, and even with the team leader. In the end, I was removed from the team.

Around that time, the leader had a friend named Ahmed, who used to provide hacked accounts with maximum levels. I became interested in having one too, but I wasn't the kind of person who would ask someone to do something for me. So instead, I bought a hacked account myself, changed its name, and eventually returned to Gangster Life.

And that was where Elsa was.

Her ID at the time was ANGEL_PARI.

I had seen her before. We had exchanged a few simple hi's and hello's, but I never really thought much about her. In fact, there was a funny misunderstanding: for some reason, I always thought she was from Multan. Every time I talked to her, I would ask, “You're from Multan, right?”

She wasn't.

She was actually from Karachi.

When I returned to the team with the new account, Elsa was there. My typing style was quite similar to that of one of her friends, and I was also calling her “Angle,” which was another thing that made her suspicious because that friend used to call her Angle too.

Because of these similarities, Elsa thought I wasn't Hamza. She believed I was actually that friend of hers. She even told me that I wasn't Hamza and that I was actually one of her friends.

That misunderstanding kept our conversation going, and somehow, that simple confusion became the beginning of our story.

And the date was 18 June 2025.

Later, when I looked back and discovered the exact timing, I found out that our conversation had started somewhere around 1:35–1:41 PM that afternoon.

I had no idea that such an ordinary conversation would become the beginning of something so important.

At the time, I was staying in Awara Gang, and Elsa would sometimes come there because there wasn't enough space for her in her own team. She would stay there with us for a while, and eventually, she even left one of her accounts in my team.

There is still much more to the beginning of this story—things that happened before and after these moments, little conversations, misunderstandings, friendships, arguments, and all the small events that eventually connected everything together.

But this was where it all began.

18 June 2025.

Not with a plan.

Not with the intention of finding someone.

Just a random decision to visit a few teams before deleting a game.
