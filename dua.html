A Private Dua

Ya Allah 🤍

Some things are better left in the hands of Allah.
✧

Ya Allah, aap sab situation jaante ho aur mera dil bhi jaante ho aur mujhse behtar jaante ho ke main kya chahta hoon. Ya Allah, tu 70 maaon se zyada pyaar karne wala hai. Na koi maa apne bachay ko ghamgeen, rota hua nahi dekh sakti, chahe woh kitna hi na-farman ho.

Main bhi tera na-farman aur gunahgaar banda hi sahi, par tera banda hoon, tera ghulam hoon, tera khadim hoon. Tu mera Badshah hai, mera Malik hai, mera Khaliq hai, mera sab kuch tu hi hai. Yeh duniya toh bas fani hai, tu hamesha hai.

Ya Allah, tera na-farman banda teri bargah mein aik chhoti si arz lekar aaya hai. Ae do jahano ke Badshah, tere khazane bohat bare hain, bohat wasee hain. Tu meri arz sun le mere Allah. Tere khazanon mein koi kami nahi aayegi.

Meri Gudiya ko salamat rakhna, theek rakhna, apni hifz-o-amaan mein rakhna aur mere liye rakhna, Ya Allah.

Mujhe wohi chahiye. Please uske ilawa meri zindagi mein kisi ko mat lana. Ya Allah, tu toh har cheez par Qadir hai na? Har cheez teri hi hai. Tu kehta hai “Kun”, toh ho jaati hai.

Ya Allah, tu Elsa ko mere naseeb mein likh de. Ya Allah, use mere naseeb mein likh de. Woh mere liye behtar hai, tu bhi use mera mehram bana de. Aur agar woh behtar nahi hai, toh use behtar bana kar mera mehram bana de.

Mere pyaare Allah, tu toh sab jaanta hai ke main Elsa se kitni muhabbat karta hoon. Meri niyyat saaf hai. Usse shaadi karna chahta hoon aur koi buri niyyat nahi thi kabhi meri.

Aur tu yeh bhi jaanta hai na ke main bas aik dafa Elsa se baat karna chahta hoon. Abhi, Ya Allah, meri baat karwa de. Aur agar yeh hamare liye behtar nahi, toh kisi bhi tarah tu us tak yeh pohcha de ya uske dil mein khayal daal de ke main usse kitni muhabbat karta hoon aur uska intezar kar raha hoon aur karunga hamesha.

Aur mere Allah, mujhe itni taqat de ke main uska intezar kar paun. Tu sab halaat jaanta hai na. Tu mera saath de, Elsa ka saath de, ke hum dono apne khwab poore kar payen. Aur jab woh aaye, toh main use khush rakh sakun.

Ya Allah, iske ilawa main sab aapke hawale karta hoon aur yeh sara mamla aapke hawale karta hoon. Aap hi koi rasta banayenge. Main sirf aap par hi bharosa karta hoon.

Kyun ke tu hi har cheez par Qadir hai. Tu sabse bara hai. Tu hi sabka nigahbaan hai. Tu hi sabse behtar jaanne wala hai. Tu hi sabke dilon ka haal jaanta hai. Tu hi behtar tadbeer karne wala hai.

Yeh hi hai tere na-farman bande ki chhoti si arz. Ya Allah, qabool farma le. Hamare pyaare Nabi Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ ke waseele aur apne mehboob ke waseele aur apni buzurgi-o-jalal ke sadqe meri dua qabool farma.

Ameen Sum Ameen 🤲🏻
Ya Rabb-ul-Alameen
