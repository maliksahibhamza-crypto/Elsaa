The Little Test 🤍
A small game before you continue...
How to play:

You will see 15 hidden boxes. Every box contains a different number of virtual points.

You can choose only 3 boxes in each attempt.

After choosing your three boxes, their hidden values will be revealed.

If the total of your three boxes is more than 50 points, you win the game.

If you lose, you can try again.

You have a maximum of 3 attempts.

The box values are randomly shuffled every attempt, so their previous positions cannot help you guess the next round.

Good luck. 🤍
