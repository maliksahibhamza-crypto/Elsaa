How it works 🔐

A sequence of symbols will appear on the screen. You will have a few seconds to remember their exact order.

Once they disappear, the symbols will be shuffled. Select them in the same order in which you saw them.

Every attempt generates a completely new sequence. You have a maximum of 3 attempts.
